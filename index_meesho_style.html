The Category Inversion: Beauty is TikTok Shop's #1 category worldwide. On Myntra, creator-led sales drive 10%+ revenue. Yet at Meesho, content commerce drives only ~3.4% of NMV despite +141% YoY content growth.
Source: Meesho Q1 FY27 & Redseer ($40B India TAM)
Sunk Cost Trap
78%
Face Upfront Sourcing Friction
18 of 23 Creators
Upfront Sourcing Friction: 78% of creators (18 of 23) felt acute friction paying upfront out-of-pocket for non-returnable BPC products. Unlike fashion hauls where clothes are returned after filming, opened cosmetics cannot be returned—eating 100% of the cost and requiring ~11 orders to break even.
Source: Primary Creator Interviews (n=23, Slide A2)
Review Integrity Gap
> 70%
Inauthentic BPC Reviews
Hover for data
Inauthentic BPC Reviews: • 70%+ (Primary n=23): Post without real testing due to 7–14 day agency deadlines and non-returnable opened cosmetics.
• 63% (Fakespot): Beauty reviews online flagged as fraudulent or heavily manipulated.
• 93% (USC Annenberg / ASCI): Creator sponsored reviews failing legal disclosure standards.
Source: Primary Creator Fieldwork (n=23), Fakespot & USC Annenberg
Creator Bottleneck
86.9%
Struggle on SKU Selection
Hover for data
Sourcing Blindspot: Creators only see generic commission % (e.g. 10%) on dashboards with zero visibility into sales velocity, leading them to promote dead stock that never sells or has high return rates.
Source: Primary Creator Fieldwork (n=23)
Buyer Trust Deficit
40%
Received Fake Cosmetics
Hover for data
Trust Deficit on PDP: 40% of online BPC buyers have encountered a fake product in the past 3 years (LocalCircles, n=4,300). 35.7% of people in our primary research showed cart abandonment due to a fear of receiving a fake product, indicating a major trust gap not only on authenticity but also on compatibility (42.9% said they are not sure if this product will match their skin or hair type).
Source: Primary Survey (n=118) & LocalCircles (n=4,300)
